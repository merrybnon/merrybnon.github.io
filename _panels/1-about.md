I'm a second year PhD student at the University of Oregon studying physics and doing research in spacial population genetics. I love being outdoors, learning new math, and reading old philosophy. If you want to really get to know me, check out my [blog](/qc/) Quiet Conversation.

**Education**  
*In progress*, PhD in Physics, University of Oregon  
2024, B.S. in Physics, Cal Poly

**Research interests**
Generally, I'm fascinated by anything at the intersection of statistical physics and evolution. Currently, my main project is using machine learning to predict how landscape structure will affect the genetic diversity of a population on that landscape. See [research](#research) for more info. In my free time I'm thinking about a generalization of the log-stable distributions. During my undergraduate, I worked on a project studying quasi-2D diffusion in lipid monolayers.
